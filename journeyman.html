What Journeyman is

Apprenticeship has one hard limit: an expert can only stand next to one person at a time. Journeyman captures that expertise once and lets it serve the whole plant — in writing, on demand, and long after retirement.

In their words

Recorded on-site from the people who actually do the work — every fix, every trick, every reason.

Written the same week

Six hours of explanation becomes a searchable record, not a shelf of unlabeled tapes.

Replayable anywhere

Walk a new hire through a task step by step — even replay it hands-free on the floor.

Plant-owned, forever

The record belongs to the plant. It survives retirement, it survives everything.

How an engagement runs

  1. A walkthrough, then a quote.

    We meet, we look at your floor, we see who carries the knowledge. You get a firm quote in days — half up front, the rest on delivery.

  2. Capture sessions, on their schedule.

    Off-the-shelf gear — a mic on the collar, a camera on the machine. The operator works normally; an AI voice asks the right question in the moment.

  3. We turn talk into a written record.

    Every session becomes a searchable document in the operator's own words. No transcription backlog, no missing context.

  4. Tweaks, then done.

    We refine until the record reads right for your floor. When it's done, it's yours.
